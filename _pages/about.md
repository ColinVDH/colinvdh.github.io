I'm a Senior Research Scientist at [Dash Social](https://www.dashsocial.com), a social media marketing platform, where I've worked since 2022.

I completed a Master's in the [Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca) at the University of Waterloo, supervised by [Edith Law](http://www.edithlaw.ca). My research there focused on human-in-the-loop machine learning, including methods for discovering the unknown unknowns of predictive models and safe reinforcement learning.
